About Me

I am completing my MSc in Investment Banking at Queen Mary University of London, graduating with Merit, following a Bachelor of Business Administration with First Class Honours from Karnatak University. My journey into finance has been shaped by a deliberate choice to pursue depth over convenience, challenge over comfort, and genuine understanding over simply getting the work done.

At Queen Mary, I approached my degree with the belief that the value of an education lies in how much you are willing to challenge yourself. Rather than choosing the easiest route, I took on a demanding selection of modules, including advanced valuation, M&A, asset management, financial modelling, derivatives, international finance and real estate finance. I also chose to demonstrate my understanding through summative examinations where available, rather than relying solely on coursework. I wanted to be able to defend my reasoning, work through problems independently and understand the principles behind the numbers and not just produce an answer.

As an Equity Research Analyst with the Queen Mary University Investment Fund (QUMMIF), I analysed listed companies, assessed their financial performance, studied industry and competitive dynamics, and translated my research into investment recommendations. I built and managed a 25-stock US value portfolio and produced 10 institutional-style equity research reports, incorporating DCF and EV/EBITDA valuation, sensitivity analysis, target prices and risk assessment. I also conducted macroeconomic and market analysis to understand how broader developments could affect individual businesses and portfolio decisions. These experiences strengthened my ability to question assumptions, distinguish business quality from market sentiment and communicate an investment thesis supported by evidence.

My work at Queen Mary extended beyond equity research. I modelled buy-side M&A transactions, working through valuation, Sources & Uses, purchase price allocation and accretion/dilution analysis. I explored acquisition rationale, transaction structure, financing implications and the financial impact of combining businesses. Through further valuation and real estate finance projects, I developed a broader understanding of how investors assess risk, price assets, evaluate returns and make capital allocation decisions. I enjoy the process of taking a complex financial problem, breaking it into its essential components and building a clear, defensible conclusion from the analysis.

I have complemented my professional training through AmplifyME’s financial markets and investment banking simulations, including simulations with Morgan Stanley and UBS. These experiences provided practical exposure to scenarios that closely reflect real world finance, allowing me to apply analytical thinking, make decisions under pressure and develop a better understanding of the demands of financial services. I was also recognised as a High Potential Candidate through AmplifyME. I have also invested in developing my practical understanding of financial markets through Bloomberg Market Concepts, Bloomberg-related market learning

Before pursuing finance, I gained experience in financial crime compliance at AXA, where I worked with customer and transactional information, supported customer and enhanced due diligence, examined potential anomalies and risk indicators, and maintained clear case documentation. This role taught me the importance of accuracy, sound judgement, attention to detail and accountability. In a regulated environment, small oversights can have significant consequences, and I learned to approach decisions methodically, challenge inconsistencies and ensure that conclusions were supported by the available evidence.

I also founded and led an e-commerce business, OVERSPEED FASHION, which I later sold. Building a business gave me a perspective that cannot be learned from a textbook alone. I was involved in sourcing, pricing, supplier relationships, inventory, fulfilment and financial management, while balancing commercial opportunities against operational and working-capital constraints. It taught me to take ownership of outcomes, make decisions with incomplete information, adapt when plans changed and understand the connection between strategy, execution and financial results. Most importantly, it gave me a lasting appreciation for the commercial realities behind the numbers.

My work ethic is built on diligence, integrity and consistency. I do not believe in taking shortcuts when the work deserves to be done properly. Whether I am analysing a company, building a financial model or taking on a new responsibility, I put in the effort to understand the details, question assumptions and deliver work I can stand behind. I value trust, take my commitments seriously and believe that strong results come from preparation, independent thinking and a willingness to keep improving.

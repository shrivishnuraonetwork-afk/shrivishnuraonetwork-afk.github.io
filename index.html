About Me

I'm pursuing an MSc in Investment Banking at Queen Mary University of London, building on a BBA (Distinction) and experience across equity research, financial crime compliance and entrepreneurship.

At QMUL I built and managed a 25-stock US value portfolio and wrote 10 institutional-style equity research reports, each with DCF and EV/EBITDA valuation, sensitivity analysis, target prices and risk assessment. I've also modelled two full buy-side M&A transactions from valuation through Sources & Uses, purchase price allocation and accretion/dilution.

Before finance, I founded and sold an e-commerce business and worked in AML due diligence at AXA. That mix taught me to question assumptions, understand what drives the numbers and communicate the conclusion clearly.
